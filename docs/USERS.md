# User Research & Personas — Deen App

This document captures user research, target audience segments, and detailed personas for the Deen App.

---

## 1. Target Audience Segments

### 1.1 Primary Segment: Urban Bangladesh Muslims (18–55)
- **Demographics:** Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna — working professionals and students.
- **Language:** Native Bengali (Bangla).
- **Prayer Behavior:** Attend mosque regularly (Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha; varying Fajr attendance).
- **Pain Point:** Frequently forget to silence phones before entering mosque; embarrassed when phone rings during congregation.
- **Tech Proficiency:** Mid-level Android users; prefer simple one-time setup over daily fiddling.

### 1.2 Secondary Segment: Global Diaspora & International Muslims
- **Demographics:** Living in UK, USA, Canada, Middle East, Australia.
- **Language:** English (primary), some bilingual with Arabic/Urdu/Hindi.
- **Prayer Behavior:** Adapt prayer times to local sunset/sunrise; vary significantly by season.
- **Pain Point:** Apps don't account for dramatic seasonal time shifts; cluttered with ads.
- **Tech Proficiency:** Higher; expect clean modern UI and privacy-first approach.

### 1.3 Tertiary Segment: Senior Mosque Regulars (50+)
- **Demographics:** Retired or semi-retired, attend all 5 daily prayers in Jamaat.
- **Language:** Native Bengali; limited English comfort.
- **Pain Point:** Complex English-heavy apps are confusing; popup ads frustrate.
- **Needs:** Large fonts, high contrast, minimal interaction, one-time setup.

## 2. Detailed Personas

### Persona 1: Tanvir (29) — The Busy Professional
- **Location:** Dhaka, Bangladesh
- **Occupation:** Software Engineer at a fintech startup
- **Device:** Samsung Galaxy A54 (Android)
- **Daily Routine:**
  - Works 9 AM – 7 PM
  - Walks 10 minutes to nearby mosque for Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib
  - Rarely attends Fajr due to late nights
- **Current Pain Point:**
  > "I rush from meetings to the mosque and always forget my phone is on full volume. Last week, my phone rang during Friday Khutbah — everyone stared at me."
- **Goals:**
  - Set silent mode for Dhuhr and Asr (mosque Jamaat times) and forget about it
  - Quick glance at phone to see "Asr in 5 minutes" without opening app
- **Tech Comfort:** High; uses VS Code daily, comfortable with apps

### Persona 2: Uncle Rafiq (56) — The Mosque Regular
- **Location:** Chittagong, Bangladesh
- **Occupation:** Retired school teacher
- **Device:** Xiaomi Redmi 12 (budget Android)
- **Daily Routine:**
  - Attends all 5 prayers at local mosque
  - Leads Taraweeh during Ramadan
- **Current Pain Point:**
  > "আমি ইংরেজি কম বুঝি। অ্যাপগুলোতে অনেক বিজ্ঞাপন আসে, আমার ছোট ছেলে একটা ক্লিক করলো আর ৫০০ টাকা চলে গেল।"
  (I don't understand English well. Apps have many ads; my grandchild clicked something and 500 Taka was deducted.)
- **Goals:**
  - Simple Bangla interface, large text
  - No ads, no accidental purchases
  - Phone automatically silences when he enters the mosque gate

### Persona 3: Nusrat (24) — The International Student
- **Location:** London, United Kingdom
- **Occupation:** MSc Computer Science student
- **Device:** Google Pixel 7
- **Daily Routine:**
  - Balances coursework with prayer times
  - Prayer times shift drastically between summer (Fajr at 2 AM!) and winter
- **Current Pain Point:**
  > "Most apps blast you with notifications every hour. I just want a clean app that tells me when to be quiet during Jamaat."
- **Goals:**
  - Automatic timezone handling
  - Modern, minimal UI
  - Ad-free experience
  - Offline functionality for subway rides

### Persona 4: Ahmed (35) — The Expat Worker
- **Location:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
- **Occupation:** Mechanical Engineer at manufacturing firm
- **Device:** OnePlus 11
- **Daily Routine:**
  - Works 6 days a week
  - Attends Dhuhr and Asr prayer at company mosque
- **Pain Point:**
  > "In summer, Dhuhr is at 12:30 PM but the mosque Jamaat is at 1:00 PM. My current app alerts at 12:30, not 12:55. I need to match the local mosque schedule."
- **Goals:**
  - Customizable Jamaat time override
  - Ability to set silent window (e.g., 12:55 PM – 1:15 PM)
